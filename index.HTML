Help & contact

Find an answer below, or reach the Registrar’s Office.

Common questions

I forgot my password

Go to Reset your password and enter your student number or email.

It says my student number is already registered

You may already have an account. Try logging in. If you never registered, contact the Registrar.

I did not get a reference number

The number appears on screen right after you register and stays there. Contact the Registrar with your student number and we will look it up.

I am a parent, faculty member or admin

Those accounts are created by the school, not through this form. Contact the Registrar to be set up.

Which mobile number should I enter?

Any number we can reach you on. Use digits only; you may add a + for a country code, or spaces.
